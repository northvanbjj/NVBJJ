About Us

North Vancouver Gracie Jiu-Jitsu

North Vancouver's Gracie Jiu-Jitsu Certified Training Center. Real-world self-defense, technical instruction, and a respectful community rooted in authentic Gracie Jiu-Jitsu.

North Vancouver Gracie Jiu-Jitsu is a Certified Training Center by Gracie University. That means you can learn everything you need to know in a challenging, fun and uplifting environment, with access to the same curriculum, training standards and methods used by Gracie Jiu-Jitsu organizations worldwide.

Gracie Jiu Jitsu teaches the most important techniques, strategies and mindsets that made Gracie Jiu Jitsu successful, first. We teach Original Gracie Jiu Jitsu all the way up through the most advanced levels, with a focus on technique, safety, discipline and confidence.

At our academy, Jiu-Jitsu is taught first as a way to defend against an attacker, later on as a way to sharpen techniques and train effectively with someone who is also skilled in Jiu-Jitsu.
