Gracie Bullyproof

Kids Ages 3–12

More than just Jiu Jitsu and martial arts for kids! We focus on Character Development throughout the year. Each Development Chapter is divided into 8-week segments where we discuss, have special challenges, and several rewards including a games day to punctuate the importance of continuous development.

Gracie Bullyproof is a landmark course for kids ages 3–12: Mat Munchkins (3–5), Little Champs (5–8), and Jr. Grapplers (8–12), teaching them the fundamental core basics of non-violent effective self-defence starting with:

  • Awareness
  • Using your voice and setting boundaries
  • When to defend yourself verbally or physically
  • All 6 character development chapters

Bullyproof Summer Camp 2026

August 10–14 · Ages 7–13 · Monday–Friday, 9 am – 12 pm at Unit 206, 250 Esplanade E. Gracie Bullyproof teaches non-violent self-defense, verbal boundary-setting, character development, and confidence — through games, role-playing, and fun. $441 + tax (1st child) with family discounts available.
